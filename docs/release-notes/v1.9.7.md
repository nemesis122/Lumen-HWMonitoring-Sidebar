# Lumen 1.9.7

- Drives now have the styles too (Ring, Minimal, Bar, Graph, Tile): the hottest drive leads, with each drive's temperature by its letter and the combined read and write rates as small figures.
- Network: the adapter's link speed (e.g. 10 Gbit/s) is a value of its own – shown in every style and switchable under Customise.
