# Lumen 4.5.3 – Transparency, readable rings and consistent colours

Stable maintenance release for the 109 designs in 4.5.2. Includes the five case
editions, 240–640 px column widths, up to six columns and six named registers,
29 font options and independent widget/sensor colour settings.

## Fixed and improved

- **Cyan Glas transparency:** the compact one/two-column sensor-card backgrounds
  now follow Glass effect → Opacity, as the wider layouts already did.
- **Case-edition transparency:** the small value-tile backgrounds, including
  fan readings and the large GPU temperature tile, follow the opacity setting.
  Text and numbers remain opaque and readable.
- **Larger rings:** Studio fan rings and the NVIDIA Edition RAM ring are 20%
  larger; Command Center voltage rings are 25% larger at their natural size.
  The number font size is unchanged. Layout fitting and available card width
  still determine the final displayed size, including on narrow sidebars.
- **Cleaner SSD defaults:** Life left, Power-on hours and Total activity are
  now off by default in the five new case editions. They remain manually
  selectable. Explicitly enabled sensor choices remain supported and saved.
  Temperature, read/write rates and used space remain available by default.
- **Immediate preview refresh:** the open Settings window refreshes its palette
  when rebuilding after colour changes or Reset all. Switching away from
  monochrome no longer leaves some design thumbnails grey until reopening.
- **Consistent RAM bars:** the additional Used-in-GB bar uses the same
  load-dependent default palette colour as the RAM percentage bar instead of
  the fixed yellow memory accent. Widget graph colours and sensor overrides
  continue to take precedence and can be reset.

The previously discussed changes to case-edition companion/info-widget
transparency and Orbit Network surfaces are not included. Their appearance
remains unchanged.

## Validation

Targeted local checks passed after each change: 326 ring/default/layout/colour
tests, 165 preview/settings/case tests and 256 RAM-colour/settings/case tests,
with zero failures. These sets overlap. The RAM regression checks low, medium
and high utilisation, widget overrides, sensor overrides and restoration.
Additional transparency checks cover glass enabled at different opacities and
glass disabled without fading the readings. The GitHub release workflow reruns
the complete repository suite and extended regressions before publishing.

## Update

Download **Lumen-4.5.3.exe** or **Lumen-4.5.3-Portable.zip** for Windows x64.
Quit the running app through its tray icon before replacing the executable.
Keep **lumen-settings.json**. No additional .NET installation is required.
Source, screenshots and SHA256SUMS.txt are included. This is a regular stable
release, not a beta or prerelease. The previous 4.5.2 release remains available.

## Änderungen auf Deutsch

- **Transparenz korrigiert:** Cyan-Glas-Sensorkarten sowie innere Messwert-Kacheln
  der fünf Case-Editionen beachten den Transparenzregler. Schrift bleibt deckend.
- **Nur Ringe vergrößert:** Lüfter in Studio und RAM in der NVIDIA Edition um
  20 %, Spannungsringe im Command Center um 25 %. Die Zahlenschrift bleibt
  unverändert; verfügbare Breite und automatische Anpassung wirken weiterhin.
- **SSD-Standardauswahl reduziert:** Restlebensdauer, Betriebsstunden und
  Gesamtaktivität sind bei den fünf neuen Editionen nicht mehr standardmäßig
  aktiv. Alle drei lassen sich weiterhin einschalten; ausdrücklich aktivierte
  Sensoren werden weiterhin gespeichert und berücksichtigt.
- **Vorschaufarben aktualisieren sich sofort:** Nach Farbwechseln und Zurücksetzen
  muss das Einstellungsfenster nicht mehr neu geöffnet werden.
- **RAM-Balken vereinheitlicht:** Der zusätzliche GB-Balken übernimmt dieselbe
  lastabhängige Standardfarbe wie die Prozentanzeige. Eigene Widget- und
  Sensorfarben bleiben wirksam.
- Alle bisherigen Funktionen bleiben enthalten: **109 Designs, 51 AIO-Designs,
  bis zu sechs Spalten und Register, 240–640 px je Spalte, 29 Schriftoptionen**
  und Farben global, pro Widget und pro Sensor.

Zum Aktualisieren Lumen über das Tray-Symbol beenden, EXE ersetzen und die
**lumen-settings.json behalten**.
